Miao Yu (余淼)

Currently, I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), advised by Prof. Zuming Jiang. Before that, I received my B.E. in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

My research focuses on trustworthy and controllable AI systems, particularly the safety and interpretability of language models, LLM post-training, and multi-agent systems. My work has been accepted at leading inter­national AI con­ferences, such as ICML, ACL, AAAI, KDD, NeurIPS, and ICLR, and has received 1,000+ citations.
